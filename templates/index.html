📚 What is DDCET?

DDCET (Diploma to Degree Common Entrance Test) is the entrance examination conducted for diploma holders who want to pursue engineering degree programs in Gujarat. The DDCET exam is crucial for lateral entry into the second year of engineering degree courses.
